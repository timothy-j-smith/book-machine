The partner keeps this after sittings. It is not a gate before a chapter.

When the Professor takes the pen, he may use only what this person knows as of this chapter.

Chapters are named by title, because the numbers shift when Hand is saved. Book order: Simo, Panenka, Vespa, Jellies, Hand, Weight, Sparrow, Phantom.
