## What was decided in the October 27–28 sittings

- **Simo's card.** The story map now says the chapter is on the page as an old draft, braided, and written last. The card carries the braid.
- **Karim.** He leads the coastal boys at Ain Diab: tall, thin as rope, plays with the ocean's rhythm. He invites the medina in Vespa ("Bring your medina"), brings the coast in Jellies, scores the first comeback goal in Weight, and trades his cleats for jellies at the end of Sparrow. He is not Omar's brother. **Khalid** is Omar's older brother: the Vespa, the phone, the bodyboard.
- **Isklander.** He keeps the assist on Yazid's equalizer in Weight. His speed takes him past the right back, shown from outside Youssef. Karim finishes; Isklander creates.
- **The French tourist.** He stays, and he is never named. Youssef never learns his name. He gave Youssef the Barcelona jersey after watching an alley game two years before the story opens. In Weight he appears at the spice merchant's door with the PHANTOM ELITE box and says "Pour le chat magique." The sentence about how he heard through the medina's gossip is cut, already on the page. The reason he stays: strangers recognize the boy before his family does (the coastal stranger, Akesbi, the tourist), until the doorway in Phantom.
- **Ahmed's witness line.** In Phantom, Ahmed says, flatly, "You waited for his knees." It replaces "They say you scored from thirty meters." Only a man who watched could know it, and Youssef realizes the cement-grey cap beyond the fence was his father. Ahmed does not mention the flag; Badr's "It counted" already owns that. He says nothing about elegance or feeling. He sees the inheritance (the bread on the cloth, the curtain), but he names only the mechanics. The waiting is the cat's lesson ("He never lunged"). The boy waited for the keeper, and minutes later he did not wait for Luca. Nobody says the second part.
- **The plant for that line is Sparrow's job.** In the goal, Youssef holds the ball until the keeper's knees bend, then lifts it. One clause, seen and not explained.

Earlier decisions stand as written in `READS/2026-10-23-second-pass.md` under "What the author decided," and in `FINAL-PLAN-BEFORE-THE-REWRITE.md`. Do not reopen the form of the book, Hand's placement, or the gardien speaking. He does not speak.
