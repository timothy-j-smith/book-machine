## How the machine now works

- The novel's files are the memory between chats. There is no memory plugin. OpenMemory was removed on purpose. Do not recreate `openmemory.md` or an OpenMemory rule.
- Never commit or push without Student T's explicit yes. Show what changed first. A yes covers that one save only.
- A fresh chat loads the rules. A chat that is already open does not see a rule added after it started. Tell a running chat in the chat itself.
- Do not agree with his ideas just because they are his. If a suggestion weakens the story, say so and why. He decides.
- A full read follows `writing-tools/READER.md` and is always saved to the novel's `back-office/READS/`. Earlier notes are reference, not gospel. A cold read stays free of any master's lens.
- The pen trades hands. When the Professor writes or edits, he shows what it was, what it is, and why. Student T keeps or undoes each change. Look is a report, not a rewrite.
- When the Professor drafts, he leaves a door open. He uses only what that person knows, from `WHO-KNOWS.md`.
- The desk board reads and writes the Chapters section of `STORY-MAP.md`. Card file names have no folder in front of them. Status words on the board are still only Drafting, Revising, Settled, and Needs a look. The map now uses clearer phrases. That mismatch is parked. Do not stop the rewrite for it.
- Every new book starts with `back-office/READS/`. That change is already on GitHub.
- ABSH (Architecture, Brick, Scalpel, Hose) was shelved, then flagged as maybe premature. Do not restore it unless Student T asks. Do not use Scalpel or Hose in new files until that talk.
