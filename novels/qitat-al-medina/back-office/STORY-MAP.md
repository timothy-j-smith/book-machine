# Story map

Acts, chapter titles, and a few sentences of what each chapter must do. One file.

The partner updates this when the *story* moves. The author does not patrol it.

Until there is a map, write the scene you can see.

## Shape

A long story, about 12,000–15,000 words. It may grow into a novella. Close third on Youssef. Eight chapters once "Hand" is written.

Book order: Simo, Panenka, Vespa, Jellies, **Hand (new)**, Weight, Sparrow, Phantom.

## Rewrite order (decided 2026-10-27)

Work backward from the climax, so each payoff is written first and each setup is then planted to fit it:

1. Sparrow (current ch06): the goal, the flag, the lunge, the free kick, Badr.
2. Weight (current ch05): the training fortnight, the first half, halftime, the comeback.
3. Phantom (current ch07): the Vespa replay, Aicha, the chest, the doorway.
4. Hand (new, between Jellies and Weight): the Grand Stade by day, Fatima by night.
5. Jellies (current ch04): the concrete pitch, Bilal, the stranger, Badr's challenge.

### 7. Phantom

- File: ch07-phantom.md
- Does: The Vespa ride home: "He saw it again." Two or three fragments (Luca's shoulder, the turf coming up, his own leg already gone; perhaps Omar's unheeded "hold"). On the footrest his right foot shifts "not toward. Away," a quarter-turn. Then, on its own line, the road ran down toward the sea. No conclusion. Youssef comes home; Aicha doesn't ask the score, only looks toward the stairs. He finds Ahmed in the second-floor bedroom, tea, the cedar chest open: only the green star catching the lamplight in the gold before Ahmed folds the cloth over it. No number 10. "Come with me." They go down to the front doorway. The minaret at the end of the alley. "They say you scored." "The Phantom": the son naming the father for the first time. The hand stops an inch away, then settles, rearranged. The mosque phrase (replaced at drafting). "But we cracked." "Practice uphill. Downhill, I never learned." Ahmed shows he was at the match by what he knows. Youssef takes his hand. No mention of the Sparrow. Keep the light, the laundry, the graffiti, the boys below, Simo, "the game went on." No ball touch.
- Eyes: Youssef
- Where: the Vespa, the stairs, the second-floor bedroom, then the front doorway
- Tension: the boy carries home the lunge; the father, who saw it, finally speaks
- Status: on the page, old draft; ending locked, not yet rewritten
