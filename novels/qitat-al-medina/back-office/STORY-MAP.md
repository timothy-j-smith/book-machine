# Story map

Acts, chapter titles, and a few sentences of what each chapter must do. One file.

The partner updates this when the *story* moves. The author does not patrol it.

Until there is a map, write the scene you can see.

## Shape

First movement of a novella. Seven chapters on the page. The count is open. Close third on Youssef.

### 7. Phantom
