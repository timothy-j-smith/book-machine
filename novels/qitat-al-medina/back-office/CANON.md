# Canon — *Qitat al-Medina*

## Timeline

- Ahmed Benali born 1986 (mosque groundbreaking)
- Age 7 at mosque completion, 1993
- Age 24 at the 2010 World Cup qualifying failure
- Age 39 in story-present (2025)
- Youssef is 11–12
- 2030 World Cup (Morocco co-host) is the dream horizon

- **Fatima, Aicha** — mother, sister
- **Omar, Khalid** — street, Vespa

## Real anchors (fiction built on these)

Timothy's Casablanca football-insights trip. Rue de la Douane. Ain Diab. Hassan II Mosque. Hay Mohammadi turf. Raja CA academy. Hadj Brahim, Yazid, Younes drawn from real people. PHANTOM ELITE is a Nike boot Timothy worked on. 2010 Atlas Lions failure is real. 2022 fourth-place run is real.

The story is fiction. Do not treat plot as biography.

## What this story is

A boy scores a goal that does not count, goes home, and stands with his father. Cobblestones, sand, turf. Three grounds. Two ghosts in the last two chapters: Sparrow and Phantom.
