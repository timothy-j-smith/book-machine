# Canon — *Qitat al-Medina*

## Timeline

- Ahmed Benali born 1986 (mosque groundbreaking)
- Age 7 at mosque completion, 1993
- Age 24 at the 2010 World Cup qualifying failure
- Age 39 in story-present (2025)
- Youssef is 11–12
- 2030 World Cup (Morocco co-host) is the dream horizon
- The match against Raja's academy is a Friday

## Names

- **Youssef** — the boy. Learns from the cats of Rue de la Douane.
- **Ahmed Benali** — The Phantom / Le Fantôme. Father. A cobblestone boy who became a national-team player, then was frozen out after 2010. Cement on his hands now. Pours the Grand Stade. First ground: the cobblestones, the same as his son's. Not the rocks below the mosque.
- **Fatima, Aicha** — mother, sister
- **Omar, Khalid** — street, Vespa
- **Karim, Younes** — coast
- **Isklander, Chams-Eddine, Yazid, Bilal** — medina side
- **Badr, Luca, Akesbi** — Raja academy. Akesbi = The Sparrow. Played with Ahmed.
- **Hadj Brahim** — rug merchant. Elder. Father figure and mentor to Ahmed. Remembers him as The Phantom. Quietly tries to coax him out of his shell. Has a cane. Knew Ahmed since Ahmed was a boy, not since they were boys.
- **The stranger on the bench** — a coastal elder, fisherman or port man, weathered by wind, sun and salt. Alone. No cane. Not Hadj Brahim. Sees the boy, not the family. Chants "Qit sihri." Appears once, briefly, in the match crowd.
- **The gardien** — Hay Mohammadi turf. The middle rung, between the free streets and the clubs. Lets the boys play after hours and leaves the lights on. At the match he is glimpsed at halftime by the Y in his shirt, never named.
- **Cats:** Simo (angles), Hamza (anticipation), Lalla (speed as absence)

## Places

- **Rue de la Douane** — the medina alley. Slopes toward the sea and the mosque. The family's apartment is above the merchants. Front doorway is the book's first and last threshold.
- **Hassan II Mosque** — the spine. Built 1986–1993. Paid for by a national public subscription. The family paid in. They have not walked the esplanade in years, though the minaret fills their window. Ain Diab's road runs past it. That is why Ahmed recoils when the beach is named, not because of the beach itself.
- **Cedar chest** — in Ahmed's second-floor bedroom. Holds the national-team jersey (gold embroidered emblem; color still open) and can hold the mosque-subscription receipt.
- **The match** — Raja training grounds, beside the public park and its cracked concrete pitch. Bleachers along the pitch. Not a separate stadium. The chain-link fence is the class line. Ahmed watches from the concrete side.

## Real anchors (fiction built on these)

Timothy's Casablanca football-insights trip. Rue de la Douane. Ain Diab. Hassan II Mosque. Hay Mohammadi turf. Raja CA academy. Hadj Brahim, Yazid, Younes drawn from real people. PHANTOM ELITE is a Nike boot Timothy worked on. 2010 Atlas Lions failure is real. 2022 fourth-place run is real.

The story is fiction. Do not treat plot as biography.

## What this story is

A boy scores a goal that does not count, goes home, and stands with his father. Cobblestones, sand, turf. Three grounds. Two ghosts in the last two chapters: Sparrow and Phantom.
