**Date:** October 31, 2026

## What changed

- **Karim's brother is gone.** Student T: a boy pushed in on the last night is a device. Eleven boys who trained together the whole fortnight are a team.
- **Eleven from the first night:** "There were eleven of them. Omar counted every night… Six from the medina. Karim and Younes and three coastal boys… Eleven. He counted again, and it was eleven again."
- **Match day repeats the count:** "Eleven. Six from the medina. Karim and Younes and the three coastal boys. Nobody else."
- **Why:** the count still works as Omar's habit, and the number is never in doubt. The pressure comes from Raja's bench of seven, not from a missing player.
