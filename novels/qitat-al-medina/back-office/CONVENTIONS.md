# Manuscript Conventions — *Qitat al-Medina*

Attach this before any line-edit. Do not "correct" these.

## Identity

- **Title:** Qitat al-Medina (Arabic قطط المدينة above the Latin on the title page)
- **Former name:** Streets of Dreams — do not revive it in the title
- **POV:** Close third on Youssef
- **Tense:** Present-feeling past
- **Dedication:** `for my football brothers` (lowercase, as written)

## Spellings (do not fix)

Youssef, Ahmed, Fatima, Aicha, Omar, Khalid, Karim, Badr, Hadj Brahim, Isklander, Chams-Eddine, Bilal, Yazid, Hamza, Lalla, Simo, Younes, Luca, Akesbi

- **Hadj** Brahim — not Hajj. Confirmed 2026-05-28.
- **Bilal** — the keeper. Formerly Anass. Do not go back.

## Vocabulary

Do not swap these across categories.

| Word  | Means                        |
| ----- | ---------------------------- |
| toes  | artistry and inheritance     |
| soles | contact and labor            |
| feet  | the world's view of the body |

`"palms of his feet"` in Simo is intentional. Do not change it to soles.

## Leave untranslated

- F'BLADI DELMOUNI
- MI ALMA VA A GANAR

No footnotes. No italics beyond what is already there.

## Leave alone

- One-word sentences, fragments, comma splices — rhythm
- `"Tap, tap, tap."` / `"Tap, Tap, Tap."` — author's beat
- `"Two Fridays arrived hot and bright"` — do not add "later"
- `"smiled a goodbye"` — keep
- `"No one in the family climbed the stairs to the roof to look at it"` — apartment stairs to their roof, not the minaret. Do not rewrite.
- The match chapter is titled **Sparrow** (current ch06; becomes ch07 when Hand is saved). The chip goal carries two images only: mother's bread, and the curtain. Do not restore the tea simile.
