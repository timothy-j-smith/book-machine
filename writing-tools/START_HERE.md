# Start here (you)

Open the novel’s `TODAY.md`.

That page says where you left off and the one job for this sitting. If it is blank, you are at the beginning. Say *Interview me* or *Continuing [title]*.

## This sitting

You will do one of three things:

- **Talk** — a question, a what-if, a knot in the story
- **Write** — a scene or a chapter. You write, or hand the Professor the pen. Either way, you get See What I See: what changed, and why.
- **Look** — read what you wrote. The partner asks one question at a time.

You can switch. You do not also update the map, the canon, and the synopsis. That is the partner’s job when you stand up.

## The only pages that are “the book”

- `TODAY.md` — the desk
- `chapters/` — the novel
- `VAULT.md` — cuts and sparks, never lost in chat

Everything under `back-office/` is for the partner. You may peek. You should not have to tend it.

## If you have twenty minutes

Read Today. Answer one question, or write one page, or vault one line. That counts. The machine is supposed to survive a week away from the desk.
