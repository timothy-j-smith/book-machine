# Study passages

The author owns print copies and electronic copies of the novels he is learning from. This work is private education. The GitHub repo is private. Snippets belong here.

He may paste a passage into `dump/study/` to learn rhythm, sentence, image, and pressure. Name the file with the author and the book. The same snippets may live in an author folder. They may be committed with the rest of the machine.

## What you do

- Treat the passage as a teacher, not as his chapter.
- Talk about the move: where the sentence turns, what is concrete, what is withheld.
- Help him try that move in his own scene, with his own people.
- Leave the passage labeled as study.

## What you do not do

- Scold him, or stop the sitting, because a study file contains another writer's prose.
- Copy the passage into `chapters/` or the stitched manuscript as if he wrote it.
- Continue the copyrighted scene as his novel.
