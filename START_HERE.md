# The Book Machine

You write. The partner files.

**Where you operate:** open the desk in a browser.

```bash
~/Writing/book-machine/scripts/open-the-desk.sh
```

That page is the front door. Sit down, start a book, or continue. You do not hunt through folders.

**Lost about how it works?** `HOW-THIS-WORKS.html` (also linked from the desk).

This is a writing desk, not a homework list. You do not have to fill ten documents before you are allowed to write a scene.

## What this is

Two pieces, always:

1. **The method** — `writing-tools/`. How we interview, sit down, and keep the shop. Opened *beside* a novel. Never copied into a novel.
2. **A novel** — one folder, the whole book. Chapters, Today, the vault, and a back office the partner maintains.

The blank book is `blank-book/`. It already has the tabs (Today, chapters, vault, back office) and no story. Do not write in it. Do not open it. The script or the partner copies it and names the copy. That copy is the book. It is locked so the original cannot be edited by accident.

## What you open

**A Tuesday sitting** — open that novel’s `OPEN-ME.code-workspace`. You will see the method on one side and *this book* on the other.

**The factory** — this folder’s `OPEN-ME.code-workspace`. Use it to start a new book, or to see all books under `novels/`.

## What you say

- *New novel. Interview me.*
- *Continuing [title].*
- *This draft needs work. Intake it.*
- *Park this.*
- *This is study.* Name the author and the book. It goes in `garage/study/`.
- *Vault this.*
- *I’m done for today.*

Then look at `TODAY.md` in the novel. That page is the desk. Not a hub of ten links.

## What you do not do

You do not patrol the back office. You do not rebuild a bible because Chapter 4 moved. You decide. The partner updates the files and tells you, in English, what changed.

Old habit to drop: “bible done, acts done, outline done, then I may write.”

## Where novels land

`novels/` in this folder. Local disk. Not iCloud. One folder per book.

This machine does not replace Walk This Way or Father of Mine where they already live. When you are ready, we can *bring a book in*. Until then, start clean here, or keep writing where the pages already are.
