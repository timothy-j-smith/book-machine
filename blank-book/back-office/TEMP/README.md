# Temp

Work in progress. Chapter versions and their notes while a pass is still moving.

Name them by chapter and version, for example `ch04-v1.md` and `ch04-v1-notes.md`. The next pass is the next number. Do not overwrite an older number.

Nothing here is the book. `chapters/` is the book. When the author keeps a version, it goes to `chapters/`, its See What I See goes to `SEE-WHAT-I-SEE/`, and that chapter's files here are deleted.
